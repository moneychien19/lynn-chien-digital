---
title: Potato Soup
---

> 中文版：[[zh/recipe/potato-soup|馬鈴薯濃湯]]

## Ingredients

- Potatoes: 3
- Bacon: 1 strip
- Onion: 1
- Water: 1000 ml
- Chicken bouillon powder: 1.5 tsp

## Steps

1. Stir-fry the onion until translucent
2. Add the bacon and stir-fry together
3. Add the water, chicken bouillon powder, and potatoes. Bring to a boil, then cook over medium heat for 20 minutes
4. Take out the potatoes, blend them into a purée, then return them to the pot and simmer over low heat for 10 minutes
