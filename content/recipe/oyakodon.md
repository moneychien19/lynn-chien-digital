---
title: Oyakodon (Chicken and Egg Bowl)
---

> 中文版：[[zh/recipe/oyakodon|親子丼]]

## Ingredients

- Boneless chicken thigh: 300-350 g
- Onion: 1
- Eggs: 3
- Cooked rice: 2 bowls
- Water: 150 ml
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Mirin: 2 tbsp
- Rice wine: 2 tbsp
- Sugar: 1 tsp

## Steps

1. Stir-fry the onion until translucent
2. Add the chicken thigh and stir-fry together
3. Add the water, soy sauce, mirin, rice wine, and sugar. Cover and simmer over medium-low heat for 6-8 minutes
4. Pour in two-thirds of the beaten eggs and gently shake the pan until the eggs are half set
5. Pour in the rest of the eggs, turn off the heat, cover, and let it sit for 1 minute
