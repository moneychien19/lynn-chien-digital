---
title: Lóo-bah-pn̄g (Braised Pork Rice)
---

> 中文版：[[zh/recipe/lóo-bah-pn̄g|滷肉飯]]

## Ingredients

- Pork belly: 500g
- Shallots: 4–6 / fried shallots: 3 tbsp
- Garlic: 3–4 cloves
- Soy sauce: 4.5 tbsp
- Thick soy sauce: 1 tbsp (regular soy sauce works too)
- Rice wine: 2 tbsp
- Rock sugar: 2.5 tbsp
- Water: 400–500 ml

## Steps

1. Put the pork belly into a dry pan with no oil and render it slowly over medium-low heat until the rendered fat turns clear
2. Add the shallots / fried shallots and stir-fry briefly
3. Add the soy sauce, thick soy sauce, rice wine, and rock sugar. Stir-fry for 1–2 minutes so the soy sauce caramelizes slightly
4. Add the water and bring to a boil, then turn the heat to the lowest setting, cover, and braise for 45–60 minutes
5. Uncover and simmer for another 10–20 minutes to reduce the sauce
