---
title: Gyūdon (Beef Bowl)
---

> 中文版：[[zh/recipe/gyūdon|牛丼]]

## Ingredients

- Thinly sliced beef: 300-350g
- Cooked rice: 2 bowls
- Onion: 1
- Soy sauce: 3 tbsp
- Mirin: 2 tbsp
- Rice wine: 1.5 tbsp
- Sugar: 1 tbsp
- Water: 150 ml

## Steps

1. In a frying pan, combine the water, soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, and onion. Simmer over medium heat for about 5–8 minutes
2. Add the beef and simmer over medium-low heat for about 3–5 minutes
3. Turn off the heat and let the beef soak in the sauce for 2–3 minutes
