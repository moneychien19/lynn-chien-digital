---
title: Gû-bah-tshá-pn̄g (Beef Fried Rice)
---

> 中文版：[[zh/recipe/gû-bah-tshá-pn̄g|牛肉炒飯]]

## Ingredients

- Beef chunks: 300-350g
- Cooked rice: 2 bowls
- Eggs: 2
- Onion: 0.5
- Garlic: 2 cloves
- Soy sauce: 2-2.5 tbsp
- Rice wine: 1 tbsp
- Oyster sauce: 1 tbsp
- Sugar: 0.5 tsp
- Salt: to taste

## Steps

1. Dice the beef into 1.5-2cm cubes. Mix with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice wine, and 1 tsp oil, and marinate for 10-15 minutes
2. Heat oil in a pan and add the beef. Sear until each side is browned, then set aside
3. Scramble the beaten eggs in oil until about 70% cooked, then set aside
4. Stir-fry the diced onion, minced garlic, and the white parts of the scallions until the onion turns translucent
5. Add the rice to the pan and stir-fry over high heat for 2-3 minutes
6. Push the rice to the sides to clear a space in the middle. Pour in 1-1.5 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, and 0.5 tsp sugar, let the soy sauce sizzle until fragrant, then toss everything with the rice
7. Return the beef, eggs, and the green parts of the scallions to the pan, stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute, and serve
