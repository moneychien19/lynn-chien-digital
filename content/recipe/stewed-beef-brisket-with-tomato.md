---
title: Stewed Beef Brisket with Tomato
---

> 中文版：[[zh/recipe/stewed-beef-brisket-with-tomato|蕃茄牛腩飯]]

## Ingredients

- Carrot: 1
- Potatoes: 2
- Onion: 1/2
- Beef chunks: 400g
- Rock sugar: 5 pieces
- Soy sauce: 3 tbsp
- Oyster sauce: 1 tbsp
- Bay leaves: a few

## Steps

1. Put the beef in a pot of cold water with ginger or rice wine, bring to a boil to remove any gamey smell, then drain
2. Stir-fry the onion until translucent
3. Add the rock sugar, soy sauce, oyster sauce, and bay leaves and stir-fry briefly
4. Add the tomatoes and carrot, pour in enough water to cover the meat, and simmer over medium-low heat for 20 minutes
5. Add the potatoes and keep simmering until they are soft
6. Turn up the heat to reduce the sauce
