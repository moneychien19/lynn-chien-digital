title: Omurice

> 中文版：[[zh/recipe/omurice|蛋包飯]]

## Ingredients

- Ham / chicken chunks / sausage / beef chunks: 100-150 g
- Eggs: 4
- Cooked rice: 2 bowls
- Ketchup: 3-4 tbsp
- Salt / black pepper: a pinch

## Steps

### Section 1

1. Cook the rice
2. Stir-fry the onion until translucent
3. Add the meat and cook through
4. Add the rice and break it up
5. Squeeze the ketchup onto an empty side of the pan and fry it for 20-30 seconds
6. Mix the ketchup into the rice
7. Season with salt / black pepper

### Section 2

1. Beat 2 eggs with salt, pour into the pan, and cook over medium-low heat
2. Take it off the heat once the bottom has set
3. Repeat steps 1-2 to make a second omelette
